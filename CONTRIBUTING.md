## Contributor License Agreement

Before we can accept your contribution, you need to sign our [CLA](CLA.md).
The CLA is the same for all eLyKseeR repositories (elykseer-ml, elykseer-cpp, elykseer-crypto):
signing it once covers them all.

### How to Sign

**Using CLA Assistant (automated on GitHub):**
When you submit your first pull request, CLA Assistant will automatically
comment asking you to sign. Just follow the link and sign with your GitHub account.

**Manual signing (GitLab, Codeberg, code.sbclab.net, or by patch):**
CLA Assistant only works on GitHub. On the mirrors at
[gitlab.com/elykseer](https://gitlab.com/elykseer),
[codeberg.org/elykseer](https://codeberg.org/elykseer) and
[code.sbclab.net/eLyKseeR](https://code.sbclab.net/eLyKseeR),
add to your first pull/merge request a commit that appends one line to
[CONTRIBUTORS.md](CONTRIBUTORS.md), and sign off that commit (`git commit -s`):

```
* Full Name <email@example.com> (@forge-username) — CLA v1.1, 2026-10-18
```

Contributing on behalf of your employer? Add the company name after your name,
and make sure you have its permission (see section 6 d) of the CLA).
